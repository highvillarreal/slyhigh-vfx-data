# Preset source notes

Presets are editable suggestions, never a claim about the active recording mode. Numeric frame rates and exposure state stay empty until entered, selected, inherited, or explicitly assigned.

Primary references checked during this redesign:

- [Sony FX30 specifications](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/e-mount-body-ilme-fx-series/ilme-fx30/specifications): APS-C sensor, E mount.
- [Sony FX3 specifications](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/camcorders-interchangeable-lens-camcorders/ilme-fx3/specifications).
- [Sony VENICE 2](https://pro.sony/en_VE/products/digital-cinema-cameras/venice2): sensor/mount variants require on-set confirmation.
- [ARRI ALEXA Mini LF](https://www.arri.com/en/camera-systems/cameras/alexa-mini-lf) and [ARRI Log C](https://www.arri.com/en/learn-help/learn-help-camera-system/image-science/log-c): distinguish LogC3 and LogC4 defaults.
- [RED KOMODO-X specifications](https://docs.red.com/955-0219/955-0219_V2.0%20Rev-B%20RED%20PS%2C%20KOMODO-X%20Operation%20Guide%20HTML/Content/C_TechSpecs/Specs_KOMODO-X.htm): RF and Z variants; mount deliberately blank.
- [Blackmagic Pocket Cinema Camera](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/uk/products/blackmagicpocketcinemacamera): Four Thirds/MFT for 4K, Super 35/EF for the listed 6K models.
- [Canon EOS C70](https://www.usa.canon.com/shop/p/eos-c70) and [EOS C400](https://www.usa.canon.com/shop/p/eos-c400?color=Black&type=New): sensor families and RF mount.

Gamma choices follow the user-specified list. Lens presets provide names and explicit focal/range values from the requested shortcuts; mount is assigned for the unambiguous Sony FE models only. Family-level presets intentionally leave the exact focal length and mount blank.
